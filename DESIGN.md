# Phase 2 visual direction

Approved: compact, light administrative pages, clear labels, simple forms, no decorative assets.

Reading: an attendance administration register for employees, guards, and administrators.
Dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

- Fixed light theme: the approved register-style layout prioritizes readable workday forms.
- System sans-serif: native Windows/Linux fonts keep controls familiar and legible.
- Dark teal accent: identifies navigation and primary actions without decorating every row.
- Compact, responsive tables: assignment history is chronological data, not a card collection.
- Clear section spacing: separates account identity, assignment actions, and history.
- Text-only identity: no logo, avatars, invented statistics, or illustration assets.
- Static layout: only functional hover, focus, loading, and error states; no animation.
- Navigation: account home, scoped employees, admin assignments/users, and POST logout only.
